Social media, nowadays, is evolving from just a personal communication platform to an essential aspect of people’s lives. Research shows that 4.88 billion people are using social media worldwide, which is equal to 60.6 percent of the global population. The most popular social media platforms are Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok has active monthly users at least a billion or more(Kemp, 2023).<br>

Social media platforms facilitate users many opportunities such as expressing their identity, connecting with people, interacting with others, validating by blue marks or likes from followers. In addition, people can connect regardless of geographical distance or different time zones, which is crucial for social interaction (Kietzmann et al., 2011).These benefits make users enjoy using social media. However, at the same time, negative effects also present. People seek validation in the form of blue marks or likes from followers. <br> 

Much content on social media reveals people’s lifestyles through their own highlight reels. Many choose to show the best aspects of their moments, with attractive photos, branded goods, and personal successes (Blease, 2015; Gonzales & Hancock, 2011; Siibak, 2009). When people see those contents repeatedly, they tend to become dissatisfied with their own lives because they have a tendency to compare themselves to others.<br>

In some instances, overusing social media causes addiction and depression. The more use of social media platforms leads more access to symptoms of depression and anxiety. (Lin et al., 2016; Vannucci et al., 2017). Research shows that individuals who use more than 10 different social media platforms are more likely to have anxiety and depression symptoms than those who use two or fewer(Primack et al., 2017).<br>

As social media continues to evolve, many users endlessly scroll through their feeds without noticing how much time has passed. Overusing social media has significant effects on individuals’ time management and daily performance. Overusing social media negatively affects daily life, such as studying or leisure activities. Spending more than 4 hours using social media leads to deviating from their plan.
